# BGMA Lagos Rush: Asset Master Pack v01

Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages site) to read it.

What it covers: every asset group on the Lagos Rush battle royale island (from blockout v029 and the concept masters), how big our map is for 24 and 50 players, how Fortnite and PUBG (incl. Livik) planned and managed their maps, and our recommendations for naming, variants and modular buildings.

Files: `LR_Asset_Groups_Breakdown_v01.md` (the breakdown as text), `LR_Asset_Register_v01.csv` (one row per asset type), `LR_AssetManagement_Research_v01.md` (studio research with sources), `LR_Blockout_v029_Inventory.json` (raw blockout counts).

Third-party game images (Epic Games, KRAFTON) are not included; the page links to their official sources.
